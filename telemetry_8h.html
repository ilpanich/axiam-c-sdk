AXIAM_TELEMETRY_REFRESH 

Around a §9 single-flight refresh.

AXIAM_TELEMETRY_CONFIG_CLAMPED 

At construction, once per clamped setting (§19.2 rule 6).

AXIAM_TELEMETRY_SSF_UNJUDGED 

When axiam_ssf_poll() returns AXIAM_OK leaving at least one SET unjudged (§19.1 ssf_unjudged, contract 1.60; §34.2 P1).

A hook's switch over this enum should keep a default: arm: §19.1 may add kinds.
