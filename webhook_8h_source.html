1/*
2 * AXIAM C SDK — Webhook signature verification (CONTRACT.md §13, T-145).
3 *
4 * AXIAM signs every webhook delivery with a Stripe-style signed timestamp:
5 *
6 * X-Axiam-Timestamp: 1785700000
7 * X-Axiam-Signature: t=1785700000,v1=<hex_lowercase_hmac_sha256>
8 * X-Axiam-Event: user.created
9 * X-Axiam-Delivery: <uuid>
10 *
11 * where `v1 = HMAC-SHA256(secret_utf8_bytes, "<timestamp>.<raw_body>")`.
12 *
13 * IMPORTANT — the body must be the EXACT raw bytes received off the wire.
14 * Never re-serialize parsed JSON before verifying: key order and whitespace
15 * changes break the MAC.
16 *
17 * `t=` (not `X-Axiam-Timestamp`) is the value covered by the MAC and is what
18 * these helpers parse; axiam_webhook_verify_headers() additionally requires
19 * the separate timestamp header, when present, to agree with it.
20 *
21 * The comparison is constant-time over the decoded MAC bytes, freshness is
22 * two-sided (a future-dated timestamp is refused just like a stale one), and
23 * every failure is a typed status that never carries the expected signature.
24 *
25 * Deliveries are at-least-once: a retry replays a valid signature inside the
26 * freshness window, so receivers MUST de-duplicate on `X-Axiam-Delivery`
27 * (exposed as axiam_webhook_event_t.delivery_id) with a short-lived seen-set.
28 */
