1/*
2 * AXIAM C SDK — account lifecycle and MFA enrolment (CONTRACT.md §25).
3 *
4 * The calls a user makes about their own account, none of which is
5 * administration: voluntary and forced TOTP enrolment, email verification, and
6 * the password-reset triple. All nine have been live server surface since
7 * before §1 was written; what they lacked was an SDK, which is exactly the
8 * divergence §1 exists to prevent, arrived at through omission.
9 *
10 * SIX OF THE NINE ARE DELIBERATELY UNAUTHENTICATED. A user who cannot log in is
11 * the entire audience for a password reset, and a user whose email is
12 * unverified may have no session at all.
13 *
14 * WHERE THE TENANT GOES. `verify_email`, `resend_verification` and
15 * `confirm_password_reset` take it as a BODY field — these are not /oauth2
16 * endpoints, so §12.1 rule 2's query-parameter convention does not reach them.
17 * `request_password_reset` accepts the workspace in slug form as well, like
18 * login.
19 */
