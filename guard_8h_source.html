1/*
2 * AXIAM C SDK — Framework-agnostic route guard (§10) and declarative
3 * authorization helpers (§11).
4 *
5 * These compose strictly on top of the §10 authentication path: the guard
6 * verifies the request's session token via JWKS (§ JWKS), then performs the
7 * §11 authorization check for the *authenticated caller* (subject propagation,
8 * §11.2). No decision caching (§11.6). Network failure fails CLOSED (503).
9 *
10 * Token verification is always AXIAM_JWT_VERIFY_STRICT (see jwks.h): signature
11 * with `alg` pinned to EdDSA before key lookup, AND lifetime (`exp` required,
12 * and `nbf` when present) AND the tenant binding AND the configured `iss` /
13 * `aud` expectations. An expired token, one minted for a different tenant of
14 * the same organization, or one from an unexpected issuer/audience is refused
15 * with AXIAM_GUARD_UNAUTHENTICATED and never reaches the authorization check.
16 *
17 * The macros AXIAM_REQUIRE_ACCESS / AXIAM_REQUIRE_AUTH / AXIAM_REQUIRE_ROLE are
18 * C's analog of the annotations other SDKs expose (§11).
19 */
