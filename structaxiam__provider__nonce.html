◆ raw

axiam_sensitive_t* axiam_provider_nonce::raw

The RAW nonce: 32 CSPRNG bytes, base64url without padding (43 ASCII characters).

Pass it to axiam_login_with_provider_id_token(). A credential (§37.6) — it is what makes holding the provider's token insufficient to spend it — so it is wrapped: axiam_sensitive_to_string() renders it redacted and the handle is zeroed on free.

◆ hashed

char* axiam_provider_nonce::hashed

SHA-256 of the bytes of raw as lower-case hexadecimal, 64 characters.

Give THIS to the provider's SDK as its nonce. Not a secret (it travels to the provider and into the token).
