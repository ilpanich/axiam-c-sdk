Detailed Description

Body of POST /api/v1/organizations/{org_id}/ca-certificates/import.

Deliberately carries no subject, validity window or key algorithm: all three are read out of the certificate itself. A caller that could name them separately could name a subject the certificate does not have, and AXIAM would enforce the claim while every relying party read the certificate.
