Detailed Description

A validated broker endpoint: everything a caller needs to open an amqps:// connection, and nothing that could open a plaintext one.

Dispose with axiam_amqps_endpoint_dispose(); safe on NULL and on a zeroed struct, so a caller may dispose unconditionally on the error path.
