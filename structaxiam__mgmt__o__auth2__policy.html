Detailed Description

OAuth 2.0 authorization-server behaviour that is a tenant's to decide (G-C, #818).

A struct of its own rather than a field on TokenPolicy because it is not a lifetime, and so the resolved setting reads by its documented name, oauth2.refresh_reuse_response.

Every member is optional, so this is a SPARSE body (27.4 rule 5): a NULL pointer – or a has_ flag left 0 – means the field is OMITTED from the request entirely, not sent as null. On a sparse update those say opposite things, and only omission means "leave it alone".

axiam_mgmt_refresh_reuse_response_t axiam_mgmt_o_auth2_policy::refresh_reuse_response

What a refused refresh-token replay does besides refusing.

Ordered: a tenant may move from refuse to revoke_session and never back while its organization's baseline is revoke_session. Published as a reference to the RefreshReuseResponse schema, so the two legal values reach the SDK generators as an enum (F4-W1-06, #823). Optional.
