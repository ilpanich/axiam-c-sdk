1/*
2 * AXIAM C SDK — JWKS fetch + Ed25519 (EdDSA) JWT verification.
3 *
4 * GET {base}/oauth2/jwks. Only EdDSA/Ed25519 keys are accepted; any other
5 * `alg` is rejected before key lookup. Signatures are verified with OpenSSL
6 * EVP_DigestVerify over a raw Ed25519 public key. The key set is cached for
7 * 300 seconds (at most; see axiam_client_config_set_jwks_cache_ttl()); when a refresh fails the
8 * held keys keep verifying until 300 s past that TTL and are never used after (§12.4 rule 2).
9 *
10 * Verification is STRICT by default (SEC-071, CONTRACT §10.1): on top of the
11 * signature, axiam_jwt_verify() enforces the access-token rules 10-12 (`typ`, `purpose`,
12 * required `kid`), the `exp`/`nbf` lifetime, binds the
13 * token's `tenant_id` claim to the client's configured tenant, checks
14 * `iss`/`aud` whenever the client was configured with an expected value, and
15 * (contract 1.51, rule 9) REFUSES a token carrying a `cnf` sender constraint
16 * — this default entry point has no transport evidence to check it against,
17 * so a certificate- or DPoP-bound token is rejected rather than silently
18 * admitted as a bearer token. axiam_jwt_verify_with_evidence() is the
19 * accept-with-evidence counterpart, for a resource server that DOES have a
20 * peer certificate to check against. The JWKS endpoint is organization-wide,
21 * so a valid signature alone does NOT imply the token was minted for this
22 * client's tenant.
23 *
24 * axiam_jwt_verify_ex() exposes the policy flags for callers deliberately
25 * implementing their own policy; AXIAM_JWT_VERIFY_SIGNATURE_ONLY names the
26 * omission at the call site and MUST NOT be used to admit a request. Under any
27 * policy other than SIGNATURE_ONLY the access-token rules (§10.1 rules 10-12:
28 * `typ`, `purpose`, required `kid`) apply as well.
29 */
