1/*
2 * AXIAM C SDK — JWKS fetch + Ed25519 (EdDSA) JWT verification.
3 *
4 * GET {base}/oauth2/jwks. Only EdDSA/Ed25519 keys are accepted; any other
5 * `alg` is rejected before key lookup. Signatures are verified with OpenSSL
6 * EVP_DigestVerify over a raw Ed25519 public key. The key set is cached for
7 * 300 seconds.
8 *
9 * Verification is STRICT by default (SEC-071, CONTRACT §10.1): on top of the
10 * signature, axiam_jwt_verify() enforces the `exp`/`nbf` lifetime, binds the
11 * token's `tenant_id` claim to the client's configured tenant, and checks
12 * `iss`/`aud` whenever the client was configured with an expected value. The
13 * JWKS endpoint is organization-wide, so a valid signature alone does NOT
14 * imply the token was minted for this client's tenant.
15 *
16 * axiam_jwt_verify_ex() exposes the policy flags for callers deliberately
17 * implementing their own policy; AXIAM_JWT_VERIFY_SIGNATURE_ONLY names the
18 * omission at the call site and MUST NOT be used to admit a request.
19 */
