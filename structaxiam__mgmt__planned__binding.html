Detailed Description

One entry in a plan: what would happen to one declared role binding.

old_resource_id/old_inherit/old_tenant_scope are the SERVER's current assignment, captured at plan time, for an AXIAM_MGMT_BINDING_UPDATE: an apply that unassigns the old binding and then fails to assign the new one restores exactly this — CONTRACT.md §27.6.1's "if the assign fails, the SDK MUST attempt to assign the previous binding again (same resource, same inherit)", with tenant_scope carried across per the same rule. Meaningless (and NULL/0) for AXIAM_MGMT_BINDING_CREATE and AXIAM_MGMT_BINDING_NOCHANGE, since there is nothing to restore.
