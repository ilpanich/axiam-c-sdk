59 *
60 * Both are OPTIONAL and default to UNSET. When unset, the corresponding claim
61 * is not checked by axiam_jwt_verify(); when set, a token whose claim is
62 * absent, of the wrong JSON type, or different MUST be rejected. Passing NULL
63 * or "" clears the expectation. The SDK never assumes an issuer or audience.
64 *
